The format

Music first.
Conversation included.

The Lounge Series puts the producer in the room. Each featured producer gets an interview followed by a live beat session, then both producers come together for a final conversation.

Most recent event

The Rich Flip

Saturday, August 15, 2026 12 PM – 8 PM Mill Hill Art Center · Macon, GA

Macon's Own Little Richard Tribute was a full-day cultural arts experience presented by Kevin "SCENE" Lewis featuring BeatKonductaz, celebrating Little Richard through hip-hop, sound design, live art, conversation and community.

The program paired a hip-hop culture panel hosted by Dumi Right with a three-round sound design battle: One Man Army, The Remix, and the Final Round. Eight producers competed, including S3oulc1ty and Jeck Da General. Ma Dukes, Toney Smith, Shawn J. Period, Boca Floja and Fresh Produce Records judged.
